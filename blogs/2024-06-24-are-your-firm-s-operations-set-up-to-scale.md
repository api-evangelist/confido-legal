In our latest Legal Case Management Jam Session, Confido Legal's Emery Wager interviewed Casita Simpson, the founder and CEO of Simpson & Associates, a company specializing in CRM design and paralegal support. One of the primary challenges faced by law firms is the potential breakdown in operational efficiency.
