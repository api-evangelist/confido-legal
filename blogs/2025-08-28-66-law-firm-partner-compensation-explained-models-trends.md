Few topics spark as much debate in the legal industry as partner compensation. How firms compensate their partners influences not only individual incentives but also firm culture, collaboration, and ultimately, profitability. In this episode of Financially Legal, Emery Wager sits down with Hugh Simons, former BCG consultant, law firm CEO, and prolific writer on law firm economics, to unpack the mechanics, challenges, and future of partner pay.
