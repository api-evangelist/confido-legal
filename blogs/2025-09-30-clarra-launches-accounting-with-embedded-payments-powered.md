Law firms know that optimizing the payment collection process is one of the most critical drivers of profitability, yet payments are too often treated as an afterthought. Clarra’s new accounting and analytics suite, now live on its litigation-focused practice management platform, tackles this head-on by embedding Confido Legal’s payment technology directly into its financial workflows.
